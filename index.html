I am an undergraduate student at Fudan Univeristy, majoring in Electronic Information Science and Technology. Currently, I am visiting Johns Hopkins University and Stanford University as a student researcher, advised by Prof. Alan L. Yuille and Dr. Jieneng Chen on spatial intelligence. I also work closely with Prof. Jingjing Chen at Fudan University.

My research lies at the intersection of embodied AI and multimodal learning, with a special focus on developing physically grounded spatial intelligence. My current research interests include physically grounded reconstruction and representation, dexterous manipulation, coding agent reasoning and multimodal generative models.

I am currently seeking Fall 2027 Ph.D. opportunities. Please feel free to contact me.
