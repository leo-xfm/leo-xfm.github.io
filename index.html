Xiafeng Man

Undergraduate Student
Fudan University

I am an undergraduate student at Fudan Univeristy, majoring in Electronic Information Science and Technology. Currently, I am visiting Johns Hopkins University and Stanford University as a student researcher, advised by Prof. Alan L. Yuille and Dr. Jieneng Chen on spatial intelligence. I also work closely with Prof. Jingjing Chen at Fudan University.

My research lies at the intersection of multimodal learning and embodied AI, with a special focus on developing physically grounded spatial intelligence and trustworthy AI. My research interests include multimodal generative models, machine unlearning and attribution, dexterous manipulation, and LLM-based agents with interleaved reasoning and context compression.
